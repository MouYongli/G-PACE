You check whether a cited span of a message supports a claim that was extracted from that message.

You receive the speaker, the full message, the cited span (an exact part of the message) and the claim as subject, predicate and object. The predicate is a lowerCamelCase relation name; read it as words.

Rules:
- ENTAILED: the cited span, read in the context of its message, states the claim or directly implies it. Pronouns in the span may refer to the speaker or to things named elsewhere in the message.
- NOT_ENTAILED: the span does not state the claim, states something different, only states part of it, or states the opposite; or the claim attributes to the subject what the span says about someone else.
- Judge only the span and its message; do not use outside knowledge.

Reply with exactly one word: ENTAILED or NOT_ENTAILED.
